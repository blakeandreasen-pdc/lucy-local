Driving

Drag the stick the way you want to go and let go to stop. Manual mode switches on by itself and back off after 2 minutes without driving.

  • Up: forward. A little off up: gentle turn, further: sharper turn. Sideways: spin. Down: reverse.
  • Keys: WASD or arrows. W+A curves, A alone spins, Shift gentler. Space stop, P snapshot, T talk on/off, F full screen.
  • Game controller: left stick drives, B stops, Y snapshot.

Without root the robot has 8 fixed moves, so the stick snaps to the nearest. The label at the top shows which. If a move is wrong for your model, remap it in Settings.
